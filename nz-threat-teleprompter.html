77% of people who were targeted by a voice clone lost money.

SLIDE 11 The 10 Actions

So what do you actually do about all of this?

Here are the 10 actions that matter most — based on what we actually see when we test NZ businesses.

1. Enable MFA everywhere. Not just for admins. Everyone. This blocks 99.9% of automated credential attacks.

2. Test your backups today. Not next month. Today. If you can't actually restore from a backup, it's not a backup.

3. Close any remote access that's exposed to the internet. RDP, VPN portals, admin panels.

4. Patch critical vulnerabilities within 48 hours. 42% of exploited vulnerabilities were weaponised before they were even publicly disclosed.

5. Review who has access. Remove ex-employees. Reduce admin accounts. Identity is the number one attack surface in 2026.

6. Configure your email security — SPF, DKIM, DMARC. Your IT person can do this in 30 minutes.

7. Train your team on deepfakes and AI phishing. Security awareness training reduces phishing success by 86% after one year.

8. Implement verbal verification for financial requests. Code words, callback procedures. This is the only reliable defence against deepfakes.

9. Create an incident response plan. Who calls who? Who pulls the plug? Who contacts the Privacy Commissioner? Figure this out now, not during a crisis.

10. Get a professional penetration test. 90% of breaches exploited basic misconfigurations that testing would have caught. Your IT team keeps systems running — a pentester finds what they miss.
